Privacy Policy

We respect your data and your audience.

This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Overview

MintiStudio helps creators turn long-form content into short-form clips. We only request the data we need to provide the service and we do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

Account details

Email, name, and login information when you sign in.

Connected platforms

OAuth tokens and channel IDs required to publish on your behalf.

Content inputs

Video URLs, uploads, and metadata used to generate clips.

Usage analytics

Basic usage logs to keep the product reliable and secure.

How we use data

We use your data to authenticate your account, connect to social platforms, generate shorts, schedule publishing, and provide performance insights. We do not use your data for unrelated advertising.

Sharing

We only share data with third-party services you explicitly connect (such as YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok). Those services process your data under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can disconnect a platform at any time, and we will remove stored tokens and identifiers. You can request full account deletion by contacting support.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at info@minticrm.com.
