Ethics Policy

Our commitment to transparent, honest, and reader-first content.

1. Editorial Independence

Our reviews and recommendations are based on independent research, testing, and expert opinion. We do not accept payment for positive reviews. Our editorial team has full control over the content we publish, and our recommendations are never influenced by advertisers or affiliate partners.

2. Affiliate Links & Monetization

We use affiliate links to earn a commission on products purchased through our links. This does not influence our editorial decisions or the price you pay. It is simply how we fund our work and keep the lights on. We clearly disclose our affiliate relationships to be fully transparent with our readers.

3. Product Testing & Review Process

Whenever possible, we conduct hands-on testing of the products we review. Our process is designed to be fair, consistent, and focused on the user's perspective. We aim to provide a balanced view, highlighting both strengths and weaknesses to help you make an informed decision.

4. Corrections & Updates

We are committed to accuracy. If we make a factual error, we will correct it promptly and transparently. We also regularly update our content—especially buying guides—to ensure it remains current, accurate, and relevant.
