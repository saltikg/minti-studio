Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of data associated with your account.

1. Request Deletion by Email

Send an email to support@mintiproduct.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.

2. Include These Details
  • The email address you used to sign in
  • The Instagram or Facebook Page you connected (name or ID if available)
  • Any relevant screenshots or error messages (optional)
3. What We Delete

We delete stored OAuth tokens, connected account identifiers, channel/page metadata, and related logs associated with your workspace, unless we are required to retain certain records for legal or security purposes.

4. Timeline

We typically process deletion requests within 30 days.
