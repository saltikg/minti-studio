## Framing that follows the people on screen

A wide horizontal shot doesn't fit a vertical screen by default. Cropping the middle often cuts someone out.

The tool frames the Short around the faces it finds:

- **One person talking** stays centered and followed through the clip.
- **Two people on screen**, as in an interview, become two stacked frames so both stay visible.
- **You can keep the original 16:9 frame** instead, if the shot works better wide.

:::tip
Interviews and podcasts with two speakers are where automatic framing saves the most time. Check the split layout on your first Short before you change anything by hand.

## What makes the AI pick a moment

Not every minute of a long video works as a Short. The moments that perform well usually share three things:

### 1. A hook in the first seconds

A question, a bold claim, or the start of a story. If the first line needs context from earlier in the video, it won't hold a new viewer.

### 2. It stands alone

Someone who has never seen you should understand it without the rest of the video.

### 3. One idea, finished

A single point, made clearly, with an ending. Most strong clips land between 30 and 60 seconds.

That is what the scores in the editor reflect. A higher score means a stronger standalone moment, but you can create any suggestion, or make your own clip from the transcript.

## Editing without a timeline

Most clip tools stop at the first result. If the cut starts a sentence too early, you're back in an editor.

Here, the transcript is the editor:

- remove a filler sentence or a false start by deleting the words
- fix a misheard word in the captions
- shorten the setup so the payoff comes first
- change the title before you regenerate

## Tips for Shorts that get watched

- **Lead with the payoff.** If the best line comes 20 seconds in, cut the setup from the transcript.
- **Keep captions on.** Many people watch Shorts with the sound off.
- **Write a title that makes a promise.** "The mistake that cost me my first 1,000 subscribers" beats "Episode 42 clip."
- **Make two or three Shorts from every upload** and post them over the following days.
- **Use your own videos.** Repurpose content you own or have permission to use. Posting other creators' clips without permission can lead to copyright claims.

## Which videos work best

Talking videos give the AI the most to work with: podcasts, interviews, lessons, talks, sermons, reviews and vlogs. Music videos or footage without speech don't.
