# MintiStudio Blog Style Guide

Use the recent published MintiStudio articles as style examples.

Structure:

- Start with a short blockquote hook.
- Include this CTA near the top: [Start free with MintiStudio](https://mintistudio.com/video_shorts/login).
- Use short paragraphs.
- Use H2 and H3 headings.
- Include a "Where MintiStudio helps" section.
- Include a simple workflow section.
- Include a "Related guides" section with 2-3 internal links from the supplied published article list.
- Close with a practical CTA.

Tone:

- Calm and practical.
- No hype.
- No exclamation marks.
- No invented statistics, quotes, customer claims, or competitor claims.
- Use direct language for solo educators, coaches, consultants, and podcasters.

Length:

- 1,200-1,800 words.
