# Minti Cover Archetypes

Use one of these archetypes for each blog cover. The writer may propose the best three letters, but code chooses the final archetype for variety.

## A. Object Still Life, No People

- A desk still life with a microphone, short-form clip cards, a small calendar, and a question mark card.
- A clean arrangement of teaching cards, a stopwatch, and stacked vertical video tiles.

## B. Workflow Landscape, No People

- A long video panel flowing into floating vertical clips and then into a weekly calendar.
- A webinar timeline branching into three scheduled short-form posts.

## C. Metaphor Object, No People

- A magnifier hovering over a video timeline with the strongest moment glowing.
- Stacked clip cards turning into a staircase toward a larger screen.

## D. Phone Close-Up, Hands Only

- Hands holding a phone while vertical clips flow out into a simple publishing path.
- A close-up phone scene with clip cards emerging from the screen into a tidy queue.

## E. Solo Creator At Work

- A solo creator at a quiet desk reviewing cards beside a camera and microphone.
- A creator standing near a camera with simple clip cards floating nearby.

## F. Educator Teaching

- An educator beside a whiteboard with lesson cards becoming short vertical clips.
- A teacher near slides and a small class silhouette while clip cards organize themselves.

## G. Coach On A Video Call

- A coach on a laptop call with a client silhouette and clip cards beside the screen.
- A coaching session view where questions turn into a tidy set of teaching video cards.

## H. Podcast Duo

- Two podcast hosts at microphones with conversation cards turning into vertical clips.
- A podcast table scene with audio waves, clip cards, and a simple weekly calendar.
