MINTI COVER STYLE, LOCKED:

Create a 16:9 editorial blog cover in MintiStudio's soft 3D illustration style. Use a warm off-white canvas (#FAF9F5) with soft dimensional objects, rounded geometry, subtle depth, matte surfaces, gentle ambient shadows, and bright diffused studio lighting.

Primary brand color: Minti mint #50CDB5. Use it selectively for content-flow ribbons, active states, progress indicators, selected objects, and small brand details. Use deep charcoal/navy #0F1720 for contrast. Burgundy #981B3F is optional and must remain under ~10% of the image, used only as a small editorial accent block, clothing accent, or supporting object. Burgundy is an optional editorial accent, not a core Minti brand color. Natural neutral object colors are allowed (wood, graphite, warm gray, cream, natural plant tones); mint is a controlled accent, not the color of every object.

Characters must be stylized soft-3D humans with simplified proportions and completely featureless faces. No eyes, mouth, detailed facial features, recognizable people, or photorealistic skin rendering.

Build one clear visual story using 2-4 primary objects. Favor video frames, vertical clips, microphones, timelines, calendars, publishing objects, and creator tools. Use a flowing mint ribbon/path when appropriate to communicate content movement.

Include a very subtle oversized Minti logo-shape motif and/or dot pattern in the background at low opacity.

Keep generous negative space. Reserve the bottom-right corner as clean empty space for a logo added later.

NO text, captions, third-party logos, fake product UI, readable interface elements, screenshots, photorealism, neon effects, visual clutter, or generic stock-SaaS styling.
