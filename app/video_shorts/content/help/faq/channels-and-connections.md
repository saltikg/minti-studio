Connect and manage the social accounts you use to publish with MintiStudio.

**Which social platforms can I connect to MintiStudio?**

MintiStudio currently supports connecting:

- YouTube
- Instagram
- Facebook Pages

You can manage these connections from the [Social Connections page](/video_shorts/social/connect) in MintiStudio.

**How do I connect my YouTube channel?**

Open the [Social Connections page](/video_shorts/social/connect) and choose the option to connect YouTube.

You will be redirected to Google to authorize MintiStudio. After authorization is complete, MintiStudio can use the connected YouTube channel for publishing and scheduling your Shorts.

**What type of Instagram account can I connect?**

Instagram publishing requires a **Business** or **Creator** account.

Personal Instagram accounts are not currently supported for publishing through MintiStudio.

**Does my Instagram account need to be connected to a Facebook Page?**

No. MintiStudio's current Instagram connection flow connects directly to your Instagram Business or Creator account.

A Facebook Page is not required to connect Instagram.

**Can I publish to my personal Facebook profile?**

No. MintiStudio connects to and publishes to **Facebook Pages**, not personal Facebook profiles.

Connect the Facebook account that has access to the Page you want to publish to.

**What happens if Facebook finds more than one Page?**

If your Facebook account has access to multiple Pages, MintiStudio needs to know which Page should be used for publishing.

If a target Page has not been selected or configured, the connection may not be completed until a specific Page is identified.

**What happens if my social connection expires?**

Some social platforms may require you to authorize MintiStudio again if access expires or is revoked.

When MintiStudio detects that a YouTube or Instagram connection needs authorization again, the [Social Connections page](/video_shorts/social/connect) may show:

`Connection expired - reconnect to keep publishing.`

Reconnect the platform to restore publishing access.

**Do I need to reconnect YouTube or Instagram every time an access token expires?**

Normally, no.

MintiStudio automatically refreshes YouTube access using the authorization granted during connection. Instagram long-lived access is also refreshed automatically when needed.

You usually only need to reconnect if the platform no longer allows MintiStudio to refresh the connection, for example after authorization has been revoked or becomes invalid.

**What happens to a scheduled post if the connection is no longer valid?**

MintiStudio needs a valid platform connection when publishing takes place.

For scheduled Instagram and Facebook posts, MintiStudio processes the post at the scheduled time. If the required connection is missing or invalid at that point, publishing can fail.

For YouTube, scheduled publishing is sent to YouTube using the selected publish time during the upload process. If MintiStudio tells you that your YouTube connection needs to be renewed, reconnect it before creating or publishing additional content.
