## Schedule a Short {#schedule-one}

:::steps
### Open the video and pick a Short
Go to `My Videos` and open the video. Your Shorts are listed on the left under `Your Clips`. Click the one you want to schedule.

### Click Schedule
The `Schedule` button is on the right, under the video preview. The `Schedule & Publish` window opens.

### Set the title, description and publish time
Check the title and description (or click `Create Description` to have AI write one). Under **YouTube**, pick the date and time. Your time zone is shown next to `Publish time` — click the pencil icon if it's not yours.

### Choose Instagram options (optional)
Turn on `Share as Reel` and/or `Share as feed video`, then choose when it goes out on Instagram: at the same time as YouTube, immediately, or at a separate time.

### Click Schedule & publish
The button is at the bottom of the window. The Short now shows **Scheduled — uploads to YouTube ~2h before publish**, with a YouTube (and Instagram) icon next to its time.
:::

## Schedule all and space them out {#schedule-all}

Have several Shorts ready? Click `Schedule all` at the top.

- **Start date/time** — when the first Short goes out.
- **Interval (hours)** — the gap between Shorts. `24` = one per day, `48` = every other day.

The window shows every date before you confirm. Click `Schedule clips`.

:::tip
Don't publish all your Shorts at once. One Short every day or every other day keeps your channel active all week and gives each Short its own chance to be picked up.
:::

## Change the date of a scheduled Short {#change-date}

You can change the date in MintiStudio until about **2 hours before** the publish time — that's when the Short is uploaded to YouTube.

:::steps
### Pick the Short
Open the video in `My Videos` and click the Short in `Your Clips`.

### Click the YouTube icon next to its time
A small box shows the scheduled date and time. Click `Unschedule`. To remove the YouTube and Instagram schedules together, click `Unschedule all` instead.

### Schedule it again
Click `Schedule` under the preview, pick the new date and time, and click `Schedule & publish`.
:::

![Click the YouTube icon to see the date and unschedule](/video_shorts/static/help/scheduling/04-unschedule-popover.png "Click the YouTube icon, then Unschedule — or Unschedule all for every platform [screenshot]")

:::key
Unscheduling doesn't delete your Short. It stays in `Your Clips`, ready to schedule again. The scheduled post always uses the last generated version of the Short.
:::

## After it's uploaded to YouTube {#youtube-studio}

About 2 hours before publish time, the Short is uploaded to your channel as a scheduled video. From then on, MintiStudio shows **Uploaded — manage changes in YouTube Studio**.

To move it, click `YouTube Studio` in that box (or open the video in YouTube Studio), then go to `Visibility` → `Schedule` and pick a new date.

## How often to post {#spacing}

- **Spread them out.** If your Shorts come from a long video, don't release them all right after it. Space them over the following days so each one gets its own moment.
- **Stay steady.** A regular rhythm — daily or every other day — usually works better than bursts.
- **Use your data.** [Video Analytics](/video_shorts/video-analytics) shows which publish times got your best early views. Schedule new Shorts into those windows.

## Common questions {#faq}

**Can I change the time after I scheduled?** Yes — unschedule the Short and schedule it again. If it's already uploaded to YouTube, change it in YouTube Studio.

**My Shorts all come out right after my long video.** Unschedule them, then use `Schedule all` with an interval of 24 or 48 hours, starting a day after your long video.

**What does "Publish now (public)" do?** It skips the schedule and makes the Short public on YouTube right away.

**I'm on Autopilot (Do it for me). Do I have to do this myself?** No. Reply to any of our emails or write to info@mintistudio.com and tell us how you'd like your Shorts spaced — we'll handle the schedule for you.
